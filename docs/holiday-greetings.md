# Holiday Greetings (built 2026-10-29, local only, not committed or deployed)

Warm email and SMS greetings for the public holidays. The Super Admins are reminded before a holiday, review who receives what and the message, change it if they like, and send. Every person's delivery is shown with a retry, and the holiday keeps its record for the year.

## What it is not connected to
Nothing. It only **reads** users, staff and contacts when somebody presses Import (and copies name, email and mobile into its own list). No other module reads or writes its tables.

## Where things are
- Pages `resources/js/Pages/HolidayGreetings/` (Dashboard, Holiday, Contacts, Settings); controllers `app/Http/Controllers/HolidayGreetings/`; models `HolidayDay`, `HolidayContact`, `HolidayDelivery`; services `app/Services/HolidayGreetings/`; job `SendHolidayGreetingJob`; mails `HolidayGreetingMail`, `HolidayReminderMail`; views `emails/holiday-greeting.blade.php`, `emails/holiday-reminder.blade.php`; styles `resources/css/holiday.css`.
- Migration `2026_12_16_100000_create_holiday_greetings` (3 tables, 9 permissions, 6 features). Run on the local DB and on the simulation DB.
- Routes all start `holiday-greetings.`; menu group "Holiday Greetings" (Holidays, Greeting List, Settings), Launcher entries come from the menu, and holidays are searchable records in the Launcher.
- Commands: `holiday-greetings:remind` (every 10 minutes), `holiday-greetings:samples --email= --sms=` (sends sample greetings to ONE address and ONE number given on the command line, never to the list).

## How it works
- **Holidays**: Tanzania's public holidays are computed per year (fixed dates, Easter, and the Islamic holidays from the tabular Islamic calendar) and saved as rows, so they can be edited. Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha and Maulid follow the moon: they are saved as "date to be confirmed" and a Super Admin confirms the date when it is announced. Saba Saba and Nane Nane are observances, switched off by default. A Super Admin can add a holiday of their own, change a date, switch one off, or count one that falls on a weekend. Rows are only ever added: an edited, switched-off or removed holiday is never brought back.
- **Saturdays and Sundays do not count**: a holiday on a Saturday or Sunday stays on the list (greyed, "Weekend, not counted") but gets no reminder and cannot be sent, unless it is counted on its own page. The setting can be switched off in Settings.
- **The reminder**: a holiday begins at 00:00 (WIMS timezone). N hours before (default 8, Settings 1 to 72) every Super Admin, and anyone given `receive-holiday-reminders`, gets an email, an SMS and a bell alert, as Settings allow. It fires once per holiday (also if the cron is late, until the holiday is over) and never for a holiday that is switched off, weekend, or already sent. The SMS has no link (the gateway refuses links); the email has a button to the holiday page.
- **The greeting list**: Import (Users, Staff, Contacts, optionally only some contact categories) shows what it would do first. Only people with an email or a mobile number are imported; a person already in the list is never added twice (matched by email, mobile, or the same user/staff/contact record) and only gets missing details filled in. A leading title (Eng., Dr., Mrs., Hon...) is split from the name and a name in capitals is written normally. Adding by hand needs Title and Name and at least one of email or mobile. Mobile numbers are always stored as +255XXXXXXXXX: typed as 0674020223, 674020223 or 255 674 020 223 they are corrected; foreign numbers are refused. Emails are stored in lower case.
- **Before sending**: the holiday page builds the two lists (email, SMS) from the active people, one row per address. If two rows ever share an address the second is reported as a repeated address and left out, so nobody gets the same message twice. Individual people can be left out of one holiday.
- **The message**: the WIMS template per holiday (16 looks: New Year, Revolution Day, Karume Day, Good Friday, Easter, Union Day, Workers' Day, Saba Saba, Nane Nane, Nyerere Day, Independence Day, Christmas, Boxing Day, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Maulid, and a general one). Placeholders `{full} {title} {name} {holiday} {year} {date} {company} {signoff}`. A paragraph starting with `>` is the highlighted wish; `|` in the headline separates the English and the Swahili line. A live preview shows the email and the SMS for any title and name. The SMS is kept to plain characters (no accents, emoji or links) so it stays one or two parts. The wording is **locked after the first send**, so the record shows what was sent.
- **Sending**: one delivery row per person and channel, each sent by its own job (after the response when the queue is `sync`). The delivery tab shows Sent / Failed / Waiting for everybody with the error, per-person Retry and "Retry all failed"; the page updates itself while messages are going out. Pressing Send again only sends to people added since. "Sent" means the mail server or the SMS gateway accepted the message: WIMS gets no read or delivery receipt from a phone or mailbox. SMS goes through WIMS Connect (the `wims_connect` and `wims_connect_sms` switches and the SMS gateway); with them off the SMS rows fail with a clear message and can be retried once SMS is on. Emails use the default mailer and show in Delivery Logs; SMS are written there too (event `holiday-greetings`).
- **The record**: every holiday of every year keeps its deliveries. Print, PDF and CSV of the record (WIMS report layout, QR to the holiday page) need `print-holiday-greetings`; each export is logged.

## The email design (third pass, 2026-10-29)
Everything is drawn in code: no photos, no GIFs, no emoji.
- **Montserrat** for every word (a Google Fonts link plus `@import`; Apple Mail, iPhone, Outlook for Mac and many Android apps load it, Gmail and Outlook for Windows fall back to Segoe UI / Helvetica / Arial).
- **The RANS logo**, with no container: dark blue on the white top of the email, the yellow "Building & Civil Contractor" bar and "Your Contractor" in orange, centred. The R and the NS are two small PNG cut-outs of the real logo (made by `resources/holiday/make_logo_parts.py` from `logo-gold.png`, filled with the dark blue); the temple between them is built in HTML and CSS (roof, beam, seven columns, two steps) so it can animate. It is the partial `emails/partials/holiday-logo.blade.php`, shared by the greeting and the reminder.
- **Flat tiles, shadows and glow**: white tiles with soft shadows, a glowing icon badge in the hero, a calendar tile for the date, a coupon-like tile for the wish, three icon tiles ("May this day bring you"), a wall of bricks and a flat footer with construction icons.
- **Icons** (`GreetingIcons`): inline SVG on a 24 grid in the colour of the text, with a plain text symbol twin for the apps that do not draw SVG (Gmail, Outlook), switched with the `u + .body` Gmail rule and an Outlook conditional.
- **Animation is CSS** (glow, float, the temple's columns and roof rebuilding, bricks being laid again every 9 to 10 seconds). It runs where the mail app runs CSS animation (Apple Mail, iPhone, Samsung Mail, Thunderbird, Outlook for Mac). Gmail and Outlook for Windows ignore it and show the finished design, which is also what a reader with reduced-motion sees.
- Every holiday uses the same template and its own colours and icons, including the days a Super Admin adds (they use the RANS greeting: dark blue, yellow and blue). The reminder email to the Super Admins (`emails/holiday-reminder`) is in the same design, in the colours of the holiday.

## The photo behind the email (2026-10-29)
- The section under the logo has a photo that reflects the holiday, **in black and white at 15% opacity, filling the whole section**: the photo in grey is laid at 15% over the colour of the hero and saved as one ordinary JPEG (`HolidayPhotos::background`, kept in `public/images/holiday/bg`, made when first needed, not in git). Karume Day and Nyerere Day use the photos supplied by the owner; the other templates use freely licensed photos from Wikimedia Commons (`resources/holiday/photos`, credits in `CREDITS.md`).
- A Super Admin (permission `manage-holiday-photos`) can change a template's photo in **Settings > Photos of the emails** or on the holiday page (under the preview): **Upload a photo** (JPG, PNG or WEBP, up to 8 MB, checked as a real picture and scaled down), **WIMS photo** (put the original back) or **No photo**. It applies to that template in every year and every holiday that uses it.
- The date is a tear-off calendar page (rings, a month header in the accent colour, the day in a regular weight, the name of the holiday under it) whose colours follow the colour of the hero.
- A mail app loads the photo from a public address: Settings has "Public address for the pictures in emails" (empty = the address of WIMS). On a local copy the address is not reachable from the internet, so a real mail shows the flat colour instead of the photo; the preview inside WIMS and the screenshots show it. Outlook for Windows ignores background pictures and shows the flat colour too.

## Permissions, features, logs, ARIS
- Permissions (group "Holiday Greetings", Super Admin has all): `read-holiday-greetings`, `manage-holidays`, `read-holiday-contacts`, `manage-holiday-contacts`, `prepare-holiday-greetings`, `send-holiday-greetings`, `print-holiday-greetings`, `manage-holiday-settings`, `receive-holiday-reminders`, `manage-holiday-photos`. Every controller action checks its permission by name.
- Global Feature Control: `holiday_greetings` plus `holiday_dashboard`, `holiday_contacts`, `holiday_send`, `holiday_reports`, `holiday_settings` (`features:reconcile` finds nothing missing for them).
- Logging: every change (holidays, people, imports, messages, left-out people, sends, retries, reminders, settings, exports) goes to the activity log under "Holiday Greetings".
- ARIS: tools `holiday_next`, `holiday_year`, `holiday_status` and native questions ("When is the next holiday?", "How many holidays are there this year?", "Were the Christmas greetings sent?"). ARIS itself still answers "still training" while `AiAssistantService::TRAINING_MODE` is on. No email, mobile or message text is ever returned.

## Limits (said plainly)
- Islamic holiday dates are estimates until confirmed. A reminder follows the date on the row.
- No automatic send: a Super Admin always presses Send. No scheduled send, no per-person language choice, no unsubscribe (the list is internal; remove or switch off a person to stop greetings).
- Only Tanzanian mobile numbers are accepted (same rule as the rest of WIMS).
- SMS delivery receipts are not available; "Sent" is the gateway accepting the message.
- Sending hundreds of emails with the `sync` queue runs after the response in the same PHP process; with a real queue worker (Settings > Performance & Data) it is faster and safer.

## Bundles, automatic sending and the year on the calendar (2026-10-29)

- **Greeting Bundles** (menu: Holiday Greetings > Greeting Bundles, route `holiday-greetings.bundles.*`, feature `holiday_contacts`, permissions `read/manage-holiday-contacts`): named groups of people from the greeting list (tables `holiday_bundles`, `holiday_bundle_contacts`). Migration `2026_12_17_100000_create_holiday_bundles` (also adds `holiday_days.auto_plan`, `holiday_days.auto_attempted_at`, `holiday_deliveries.bundle_name`).
- **Sending**: "Send the Greetings" opens a window that asks which list receives it (whole greeting list or a bundle, with people / emails / SMS / not-yet-sent counts). A holiday can be sent to several bundles in turn; nobody is sent the same greeting twice. The delivery rows keep the bundle name, and the holiday page shows "Sent to: ...". The "Who receives it" tab can be filtered by bundle.
- **Automatic sending**: per holiday ("Automatic Sending" button) or as the default in Settings (off until turned on). Minutes before the holiday begins (00:00): presets 0, 10, 30 minutes, 1, 3, 8 hours, 1, 2 days, or any value up to 7 days; plus the bundle. `holiday-greetings:auto-send` runs every minute (scheduler), once per holiday (`auto_attempted_at`), only for a holiday that counts and is not yet sent, never after the holiday day is over. Super Admins get a bell alert, the reminder says when it will send itself. A bundle chosen for an automatic send cannot be removed.
- **Email**: the year sits between the two rings of the calendar icon.

## Schedule Greetings and past holidays (2026-10-30)

- "Automatic Sending" is now called **Schedule Greetings** everywhere (holiday page, Settings, alerts, activity log).
- A holiday whose day is over (`HolidayDay::isPast()`, `past` in the holiday row) can still be opened and read, but **Send the Greetings** and **Schedule Greetings** are disabled and the server refuses both (`SendController::start` and `auto`).
