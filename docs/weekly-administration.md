# Administration (weekly document)

Built 2026-10-20. Routes `weekly-administration.*`, features `weekly_administration`, `administration_charity`, `administration_reports`, workflow (Alerts) module `administration`, log names `Administration` and `Administration Charity`.

## What it does
- One document per week: numbered lines (No, Description, Beneficiary, Amount), an optional Charity section, a total (lines + charity when included).
- Three stages: **Requested** (prepared) -> **Approved** (`approve-weekly-administration`) -> **Authorized** (`authorize-weekly-administration`). Reject at either stage with a reason; the maker corrects it and it goes back to Requested. Cancel needs a reason.
- Separation: the maker cannot approve or authorize their own document, and the approver cannot also authorize it (a Super Admin may).
- Start from the **template** (the standing items, in order, with usual beneficiary and amount) or from **zero**. Lines can be added, removed and moved. Every typed line is remembered as an item (same name = same item) so reports can follow it.
- **Charity** sub-module: a list of people / places with a monthly amount. A document pulls the active ones (wording `CHARITY - :name FOR :month`, set in Settings); each pulled amount and wording is editable on the document without changing the list. Including or removing the section changes the document total.
- **Duplicate**: needs `duplicate-weekly-administration` + `create-weekly-administration` and the password of the person doing it (not only a Super Admin). The copy keeps lines and charity, the date moves one week on (never into the past), and it starts again at Requested.
- Beneficiary ("Requested by") defaults to the person making the document and can be any active WIMS user. The date defaults to today.
- **Send** (`send-weekly-administration`): PDF with QR attached, to typed emails (comma separated, max 15) and/or all active Super Admins; each sending is kept on the document and logged.
- Print / PDF in the WIMS layout (letterhead, watermark, QR, signatures: Requested / Approved / Authorized).
- Reports (8): register, expenses by item, item month by month, every line, totals by month, by beneficiary, charity detail, charity by person. Filters: dates (this month / last month / quarter / year shortcuts), status, item, beneficiary, requested by, section, charity, amount range, search. Money reports count Authorized documents unless a status is chosen. Print, PDF, Excel, CSV; every export logged.

## Permissions (Roles page, group "Administration (Weekly)")
read / create / update / approve / authorize / cancel / duplicate / print / send / view-all, `manage-administration-items`, `read-administration-charity`, `manage-administration-charity`, `manage-administration-settings`. Only Super Admin has them after the migration. Without `view-all-weekly-administration` (or approve/authorize) a person sees only documents they made or are the beneficiary of.

## Logging
Every action writes the activity log (requested, updated, approved, authorized, rejected, cancelled, duplicated, refused duplicate password, sent by email, printed, downloaded, report exported, charity pulled, item and charity changes). Prints and downloads also land in Downloads & Prints automatically. Smart Brief reads these under its own module `weekly_administration` (needs `read-weekly-administration` / `read-administration-charity`). QR kind `admin_document`; Launcher entries `administration` and `administration-charity`; menu group "Administration".

## Payment (added 2026-10-20)
An **Authorized** document is paid in two ways that share one ledger (`admin_document_payments` + `admin_document_payment_lines`; what a line has been paid is the sum of its payment lines):
- **Mark paid** (`mark-paid-weekly-administration`): all remaining lines at once, the chosen lines, or part of a line, to a chosen **user or staff member** (date, method, reference, notes). Recorded at once with the name of the person. Reversible with a reason (`reverse-payments-weekly-administration`).
- **Payment Voucher** (`create-payment-voucher-from-administration`): a Draft voucher (type Administration, payee the staff member or the user's name, one voucher line per document line) is made and the amount is held ("Pending Voucher"). It becomes a real payment when the voucher is marked paid in Payment Vouchers; a rejected / cancelled / deleted voucher releases it. Editing the voucher cannot change its amount.
Line status: Unpaid, Partially Paid, Paid (or Pending Voucher while held); the document is Unpaid, Partially Paid or Paid. A document with payments cannot be cancelled until they are reversed / released. Alerts events: `partially_paid`, `fully_paid`, `voucher_requested`, `voucher_released`, `payment_reversed`; the creation alert (`requested`) is on for bell, SMS and email by default. Report "Payments made"; the register has Paid / Balance / Payment and a payment filter. Migration `2026_11_29_100000`.

### Paying one person, per-beneficiary vouchers, and the voucher rule (added 2026-10-20)
- **One person who distributes:** "Mark all paid" (or "Voucher for all left") with the option "This person gives it to the beneficiaries": the whole document is paid / vouchered to ONE user or staff member, who hands the cash out offline. The payment is flagged and shown as such.
- **Vouchers per beneficiary:** one voucher for each beneficiary (lines grouped by the Beneficiary column; charity lines by person or place; a beneficiary who is a staff member or user gets a voucher in that name), for exactly their amount, all in ONE Payment Voucher Batch and already Submitted, so "Approve" / "Approve and authorise" on the batch page does them all together. Route `weekly-administration.payments.vouchers`.
- **Vouchers start only here:** voucher type **Weekly Administration** is refused by the Payment Vouchers module (new voucher, edit of another voucher, the batch screen) and is not offered in its type list; a voucher of this type cannot be changed to another type, and its amount follows the document lines.

### Categories and staff members (added 2026-10-20, migration 2026_11_30_100000)
Every item has a **category** (Fuel, Cleaning, Allowance, ... the list is soft-coded in Settings; any category already used is kept; typing a new one adds it) and an optional **staff member**. A line keeps both as they were when it was made (so changing an item later never rewrites old documents); a line can be given its own category / staff member in the form ("Category / staff member"). Items made before get a guessed category (`AdminCategories::guess`) and are linked to the staff member whose name is exactly the usual beneficiary (`AdminCategories::backfill`). The charity section counts as the category Charity and is never linked to staff.
Reports: new "Expenses by category", "Category expenses month by month", "Expenses by staff member"; the Category and Staff member filters are on the item, line, beneficiary and register reports and on the document list; the item and line reports show both as columns.

### Staff / Contact (added 2026-10-20, migration 2026_12_02_100000)
"Staff member" became **Staff / Contact** everywhere (items, document lines, the document list filter, the item list filter, every report filter, print, PDF and Excel). It is one searchable dropdown in two sections like Projects / Warehouses: **Staff** first (all staff on the payroll; filters and reports list every staff member, also those who left), **Contacts** second (values `s:ID` / `c:ID`, `AdminCategories::peopleOptions()` and `parsePerson()`). A line or item is linked to a staff member OR a contact (staff wins if both are sent); choosing one fills the usual beneficiary. Filter query field is `person` (plain `staff_id` / `contact_id` still work in links). "Expenses by staff member" is now "Expenses by staff / contact" with a Type column.

### Headquarters, redesigned dialogs, QR on rows (added 2026-10-20, migration 2026_12_01_100000)
Every document is for one **headquarters** (Unguja HQ, Pemba HQ ... soft-coded in Settings, `admin_hqs`), chosen FIRST on the form and refused when blank or not on the list (an HQ already used on a document stays valid). It shows on the list, the document, the print, the report rows, and is a filter on the list, the charity page and every report; new report "Totals by headquarters". Charity people can belong to one HQ (blank = every HQ): a document pulls the people of its own HQ plus the shared ones. The HQ is in the title of the app bell, in every SMS text (#hq) and email subject / body of the Alerts page, in the voucher notes and in the email subject of a sent document. Choosing a staff member on an item or a line fills the usual beneficiary with their name (a typed name is kept). QR button: icon on each list row (`QrRecordButton` accepts `record` and `compact`) plus the one on the document page.
